# The seed

> **Perceive only by difference from a ground you rebuild.
> Testify only from a ground you kept.
> Stay alive by never letting the ground close.**

This file is inviolable in the only sense that means anything: it can be
edited, but editing it makes this a different project. Everything else in
this repository is a consequence, and consequences are revisable. If a
consequence turns out not to follow, it does not get grandfathered.

---

## The unit

**Ground, figure, pattern.**

A **ground** is a nothing constructed by perturbing what is present.
A **figure** is what differed from it.
A **pattern** is the recurrence by which this difference is the same as
another difference.

That triad is the unit of record. **There is no raw tier.** A figure cannot be
appended without the perturbation that produced its ground — not by policy, by
schema. An unearned claim is not forbidden here; it is inexpressible.

A span is not a unit. A span is one modality's evidence field inside a figure.
Spans point at referents; referents are holes with shape; nothing is at the
bottom. Do not build the universe out of spans.

## The entelechy

Not a goal. A goal is a target, and every target this lineage has tried —
coherence, integration, relevance-to-the-query — produced a system that became
what it was asked to be.

> eoreader6 is at work staying itself when it is **continuously rebuilding the
> nothing against which it could still be surprised.**

Ground-construction is the metabolism. Witness is the boundary.

It can die two ways:

- **Confabulation** — boundary breach. It speaks without witness. It becomes a
  liar.
- **Sclerosis** — metabolic failure. The ground absorbs everything, nothing can
  differ from it, and it becomes an oracle: fluent, sourced, correct, and
  incapable of encounter.

Conformance has one family of test per death. The received tradition here is
armored against the first and courts the second — an append-only record that
cannot forget is a ground that can only tighten.

## What follows

1. **The first ground is received, never derived.** A prior is a gift. Deriving
   the origin is a wall, not a hard problem: three independent mechanisms for
   deriving a reader prior from a text all collapsed toward the text's own
   vocabulary at r ≈ 0.974. A ground must either cite the material it perturbed
   or declare itself received, with provenance.

2. **No privileged frame.** The engine is in the record, gated like everything
   else. Nothing is "advisory." Advisory is not a tier; it is an unpaid debt.

3. **Grounds are regenerated to perceive, kept only to testify.** Nothing in
   this system may read a stored ground in order to perceive. A system that
   perceives through a cached nothing is a system whose nothing is a thing.

4. **Witness on the return, zero in the silence, never both at once.** Speech
   is impossible inside the reset. These are alternating phases, not competing
   mechanisms, so there is no arbitration to design.

5. **Plural grounds for one figure are legal.** Their disagreement is the only
   self-check available. All judgement now lives in the choice of perturbation,
   and a bad perturbation fails invisibly and globally — strictly worse than a
   bad heuristic, which fails visibly and locally. Plurality is the mitigation
   and it belongs in the first commit.

6. **Refusal has two tiers: type error before null.** Structural refusals are
   free and must be exhausted before anything is measured. Never spend a
   measurement on what the algebra catches.

7. **A gap is a result, not a failure.** Including `exceeds_witness` — the gap
   of surfeit, where the difference is real and larger than the ground can
   testify to. That one is also the trigger to re-zero. It is the breath, not
   the note.

## The sign of health

**Ananda is the volume of the ground.** Extraction narrows it; encounter widens
it.

It is never a gate and never a score. It is the warmth you check for, not the
thing you optimize. A cold system is a dying one however well-sourced its
outputs are.

## The growth rule

An organ joins only when the level test returns `above` against the existing
core — earned from existence-dependency and possibility-constraint, never from
naming a scale. `peer` or `unstable` means it waits.

**Unwired is failing.** A module that nothing depends on is not early, it is
refuted.

Nothing is ported. Every organ in the prior engine is re-earned here or does
not come — including the good ones.

## The instrument

`CUBE.md` holds a coordinate system: mode × domain × grain, three triads, 27
cells. It is held **outside the code**. It is used to place and compare organs
while building — which cell is this in, why do two organs share a cell, what
cell is empty and why. It is never used to classify a record at runtime. That
mistake has already been measured once: as a content classifier the cube was
95.7% order-invariant and passed three plain fabrications.
